### 1. App Purpose Description
BuildTape Pro is a professional construction calculator and jobsite estimating tool designed for carpenters, framers, contractors, and builders. It solves the everyday jobsite problem of complex imperial fraction calculations, stair layout geometry (risers, treads, and stringers), roof rafter pitch cuts (common, hip/valley), and material estimation (studs, concrete volume, drywall, board feet). It operates completely offline so tradespeople can rely on it on remote jobsites without internet access.

### 2. Access Instructions and Test Credentials
- No account registration or login is required. The app is 100% functional without an account.
- Free Tier Features: Full imperial tape calculator, calculation history tape, and basic job saving.
- Pro Gated Features: Stair Solver, Rafter Solver, and Material Estimators.
- How to Test Pro: Tap the "Settings" tab at the bottom right. The reviewer can tap "Unlock Lifetime Pro" using Apple's Sandbox / In-App Purchase test environment. Alternatively, tap "Restore Purchases" if testing an account with prior sandbox purchases.
