❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Does BuildTape Pro require an active internet connection?
No. BuildTape Pro runs 100% offline. All fraction math, stair calculations, roof rafter pitches, and material estimations are performed locally on your device. You can use it in basements, rural framing sites, and off-grid locations without cellular reception.
What is included in the Free Tier vs. Lifetime Pro?
The Free Tier gives you full access to the Imperial Tape Measure & Fraction Calculator, running tape history, and unit conversions. The Lifetime Pro upgrade unlocks specialized trade solvers: IBC-Compliant Stair Solver, Roof & Rafter Pitch Cuts, and Material Estimators (Studs, Drywall Sheets, Concrete Volume, Board Feet).
Is BuildTape Pro a subscription?
No! BuildTape Pro does NOT charge monthly or annual recurring fees. It is a single one-time purchase ($9.99) that grants lifetime access to all current and upcoming pro solvers. Buy once, own forever.
How does the Stair Solver verify code compliance?
The solver adheres to International Building Code (IBC) guidelines (maximum 7-3/4" riser height, minimum 10" tread depth). If an entered dimension falls outside safe limits, the app flags a visual compliance warning before you cut your stringers.
Can I switch between Imperial and Metric units?
Yes. Navigate to Settings inside the app to toggle between Imperial (feet, inches, 1/16" fractions) and Metric (millimeters, centimeters, meters) modes.

🔄 Restore Purchases Information

If you reinstalled the app, bought a new iPhone, or upgraded your iPad, you do never need to pay again. Your Lifetime Pro license is securely tied to your Apple ID.

1
Open BuildTape Pro Ensure you are signed in to the App Store with the same Apple ID used to purchase Lifetime Pro.
2
Navigate to Settings Tap the "Settings" tab located in the bottom navigation bar.
3
Tap "Restore Purchases" Scroll to the BuildTape Pro card and tap the "Restore Purchases" button. Apple StoreKit will verify your receipt and immediately re-unlock all Pro solvers.

If you encounter any issues restoring your purchase, contact us at p0wzcb@gmail.com with your Apple purchase receipt number.
